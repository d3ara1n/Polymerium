# Polymerium — Make it yours

## Intent
Create an extraordinary flagship motion graphics promotional film rooted in Polymerium's real interface, branding, and capabilities. Minimum 15 seconds; user grants full creative freedom over duration, concept, pacing, typography, and sound and requests a finished film. Execute through final local rendering without additional review gates.

## Direction
32 seconds at 1920 × 1080, 60 fps. Amber #EEA93B, dark brown, warm ivory. Expressive oversized Geist typography, block geometry, real product screenshots, a repeating interlock motif. Original instrumental electronic score and synchronized sound design; no narration. Brand reveal is the final payoff.

## Assets
Repository brand SVGs and existing screenshots are primary sources. UI crops retain actual interface content. The shared-cache scene uses HTML/CSS instance cards reconstructed in the film's style with names, loaders, versions, and known source stamps from the screenshots. The approved Minecraft scene is original block artwork with a grass island, cabin, oak tree, pond, crafting table, and furnace, animated directly as SVG geometry.

## Authorization and scope
User delegates creative decisions and requests complete output. Work confined to videos/polymerium-make-it-yours. Do not modify app code, install global packages, update global skills, publish, or commit. Installed skills are used without global refresh to respect project-only writes. Output includes master MP4, poster, contact sheet, and editable HyperFrames source.
