## Continuity

All ordinary boundaries travel LEFT; the final boundary travels UP. `ledger.json` is generated from the cue grid before the master seam block is stamped. Main-timeline wrappers own seam transforms; mounted sub-compositions own local choreography. The camera and shot backgrounds travel together; only one scene is visible per frame.

The global progress rail and rolling elapsed-time indicator continue across the whole film. There are no repeated scene headers, footers or page numbers.
